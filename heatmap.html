Tweet Activity Heatmap

When does misinformation spread? — Hour-by-Day analysis of 10,000 tweets (Jan–Apr 2023)

Task this visualization answers: At what specific days and times do users in this network post most actively? This helps identify the optimal windows for misinformation to spread and when monitoring efforts should be concentrated.

What this heatmap reveals:

Saturday has the lowest overall activity — consistent across most hours of the day, suggesting the network is more active on weekdays.
Tuesday 9AM and Wednesday 3PM show the highest single-cell tweet counts — peak windows for potential misinformation bursts.
Activity is surprisingly uniform across all 24 hours, suggesting this network has globally distributed users rather than a single timezone.
Sunday evenings (5–9 PM) show a slight uptick, consistent with users engaging with content before the workweek starts.
