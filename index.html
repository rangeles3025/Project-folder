Key Findings

What the data revealed:

Most Influential User: pjohnson ranks #1 in both degree and betweenness centrality, meaning they have the most connections AND act as a bridge between different communities.
96 Communities Detected: The Louvain algorithm identified 96 distinct clusters of users who frequently retweet each other, shown as different colors in the network.
Steady Spread: Tweet activity was consistent from January to April 2023, with approximately 2,000 tweets per month showing sustained engagement.
Business Hours Activity: Misinformation spreads slightly more during business hours (9am-5pm), suggesting users engage with content during work breaks.
cwilliams: Tops eigenvector centrality, meaning they are connected to other highly influential users — a key amplifier in the network.
