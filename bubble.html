Top 20 users — Retweets vs. Likes vs. Number of Tweets Posted

Task this visualization answers: Among the most retweeted users, which ones also generate high likes — signaling genuine influence vs. mechanical amplification? Bubble size represents the number of tweets each user posted. Users with large bubbles, high retweets, and high likes are the most effective spreaders of content.

What the bubble chart reveals:

pjohnson dominates in both retweets (362) and likes (351) — the only user achieving top scores in both dimensions, confirming their status as the most influential spreader.
amiller and udavis have disproportionately high likes relative to retweets — suggesting their content is more appreciated than amplified, a different influence pattern.
wmitchell and davidsmith have high retweets but low likes — their content spreads widely but may not resonate emotionally, a pattern sometimes associated with coordinated amplification.
Bubble size reveals efficiency: pjohnson achieved the highest reach with only 6 tweets — the most efficient spreader in the dataset.
